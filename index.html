The Method

The Edo Editing
Process

Six steps, from first brief to delivered winner. How we turn your footage, your audience, and your goals into content that actually performs.

Book a call

  1. Brand & Audience Research

    We study your brand, your target audience, and your top-performing content. If you already have footage or past wins, we fold those in from day one so nothing starts from scratch.

  2. Creative Strategy

    We plan the edit structure built to perform, not just look good. Data and audience psychology decide the hook, the pacing, and the reason someone keeps watching.

  3. Raw Footage Review

    Whether UGC, long-form, ad creative, or motion graphics - we review all footage and assets and select the best material to work with.

  4. Precision Editing

    Every cut is tuned to the persona it targets - pacing, captions, sound design, and motion graphics - so the edit converts, not just entertains.

  5. Analysis & Iteration

    Every video is judged against your benchmarks. What worked feeds the next brief, what did not gets cut, and the next batch ships within days.

  6. Scale & Deliver

    We find the formats that win and scale output while keeping quality consistent, so your content engine never runs dry.
